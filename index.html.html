Questions we often hear

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for most services, but booking guarantees your slot, especially on weekends and wedding season.

Do you do bridal makeup at home or at the venue?

Yes. Our artists travel across Meerut and nearby cities. Travel charges depend on distance.

Is there a trial for bridal makeup?

Every bridal package includes a trial session so you can finalise the look well before the big day.

Which brands do you use?

Only professional, skin-tested brands. Tell us about allergies and we will adjust the products.

How can I pay?

Cash, UPI and all major cards are accepted.
